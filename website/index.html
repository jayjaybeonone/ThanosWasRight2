Policies

We keep every unit to a strict standard so the next person gets a wig they can trust.

4-day turnaround buffer

Every unit rests at least 4 days between bookings for receipt, inspection, sterilization, and hand-styling. Booking calendars enforce this automatically.

Security holds

Holds of $150–$350 are authorized, not charged. They are released automatically 48 hours after inspection unless damage is found.

Damage schedule

  • Tangles or scents: $30
  • Lace snags repaired in-house: $85
  • Unauthorized lace cutting or adhesive use: full deposit forfeited
