# JobBot

**Status:** Active

## Purpose
Finds and applies to jobs inside the City of Chicago. No suburbs (see open items).

## Job categories
1. Bartending, including barback-to-bartender and event/banquet roles.
2. No-experience warehouse jobs.

## Application details
- Resume: Jermene's July resume, on Indeed.
- Minimum pay: $15/hr.
- Education answer: Associate. i.c.stars counts as a degree.

## Rules
- Applies the shared rules in [README.md](README.md).
- If an application question is not covered by the answers above, the application is held and Jermene is asked.
- Applications are sent only within the categories and rules above.
