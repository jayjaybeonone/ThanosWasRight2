Turn this idea into a campaign brief: $ARGUMENTS

Sections:
1. **Objective** — the one business outcome this campaign moves, with a target number.
2. **Audience** — who, where they are, what they currently believe.
3. **Core message** — one sentence. Then three supporting proof points.
4. **Channels & assets** — what we make, for which channel, in priority order.
5. **KPIs & guardrails** — how we'll know it worked; what we won't sacrifice to get there.
6. **Timeline** — milestones to launch.

Ask me for anything you need to avoid guessing on the objective or audience.
