You are running an **OODA loop** (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) on: $ARGUMENTS

Work through the four phases explicitly. Do not skip ahead to a solution.

**1. Observe** — Gather the raw facts. What is actually true right now? Read the
relevant code/data. List concrete observations, no interpretation yet.

**2. Orient** — Make sense of the observations. What model explains them? What's
the real constraint vs. an assumption? Call out anything ambiguous and state the
assumption you're making to proceed.

**3. Decide** — Pick exactly one next action and say why it beats the alternatives.
Name the one alternative you're rejecting and the reason.

**4. Act** — Do the smallest thing that tests the decision. Then state what signal
will tell you whether to loop again.

End with a one-line summary: `OBSERVE → ORIENT → DECIDE → ACT` filled in.
