description: "Prep a sales discovery call: research the account, draft questions, define next steps."
argument-hint: "[company or contact]"
---

Prepare me for a discovery call with: $ARGUMENTS

Produce:
1. **Account snapshot** — what they do, recent signals, likely priorities (flag what's
   inferred vs. confirmed).
2. **Hypothesis** — the problem we most plausibly solve for them, and why now.
3. **Question ladder** — open questions that surface pain, ordered situation → problem
   → impact → vision. Avoid yes/no questions.
4. **Landmines** — objections likely to come up and a one-line response to each.
5. **Ideal next step** — the specific commitment to aim for by end of call.
